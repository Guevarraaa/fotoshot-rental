## 1. Required Documents

Before a booking can be approved, the renter must submit the following:

- Two (2) valid government-issued photo IDs
- Selfie holding the valid ID(s)
- Three (3) specimen signatures
- Three (3) recent billing statements as proof of address

- Payment screenshot, if paid through GCash or Maya

No signature may result in the rental application being declined.

## 2. Rental Form

The renter must complete the FotoShot Camera Rental Form with true and accurate personal, contact, rental, emergency contact, and optional student information.

Providing false information may result in cancellation, refusal of service, future rental disqualification, or legal action when applicable.

- Pick-up time: **11:00 AM**
- Return time: **Next day by 10:00 AM**
