# FotoShot Privacy Policy

> Note: This is a practical MVP privacy policy draft. Have it reviewed by a qualified legal professional before public launch.

## 1. Introduction

FotoShot respects the privacy of its renters. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and how we protect it.

By submitting a booking request through the FotoShot website, you agree to the collection and use of your information as described in this policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

- Full name
- Complete address
- Contact number
- Facebook name
- Instagram username
- Emergency contact name, number, and relationship
- Student information, if provided
- Rental details
- Payment method
- Payment screenshot
- Two valid government-issued photo IDs
- Selfie holding valid ID(s)
- Three specimen signatures
- Three recent billing statements as proof of address
- Digital signature
- Printed name
- Date signed

## 3. Why We Collect This Information

We collect this information to:

- Verify renter identity
- Review and approve rental requests
- Confirm payment
- Protect the rented camera and accessories
- Contact the renter about the booking
- Contact the emergency contact if needed
- Enforce rental agreement terms
- Handle disputes, damages, loss, theft, or late returns

## 4. Payment Information

For GCash or Maya payments, FotoShot only collects the uploaded payment screenshot. FotoShot does not collect bank card numbers or online banking passwords.

## 5. File Uploads

Uploaded IDs, billing statements, specimen signatures, selfie photos, and payment screenshots are considered sensitive information. These files are stored in private storage and are intended to be accessed only by authorized FotoShot admin users.

## 6. Data Sharing

FotoShot does not sell renter data.

Information may only be shared when necessary:

- With the renter's consent
- To comply with legal requirements
- To enforce rental terms
- To respond to loss, theft, damage, fraud, or false identification

## 7. Data Security

FotoShot will use reasonable security measures, including:

- Admin authentication
- Private file storage
- Restricted access policies
- Secure database rules
- Limited admin access

However, no online system is 100% secure.

## 8. Data Retention

FotoShot may keep booking records and uploaded documents as needed for rental operations, dispute handling, and legal or business record purposes.

A formal retention schedule may be added in a future version.

## 9. Customer Rights

Customers may contact FotoShot to ask about their submitted information or request correction of inaccurate information.
